Audio and video processing

The app reduces background noise in spoken audio and videos on your iPhone. You choose files from Files or Photos. The app does not record from your microphone or upload your recordings for processing. Cleaning works offline once the source file is on your device. Downloading a source from iCloud or another provider may require an internet connection.

Results depend on the source recording and the selected cleaning strength. Complete removal of noise, echo or distortion is not guaranteed. Music and other non-speech sounds may be reduced or altered. The app is not an audio restoration service that can recover every damaged recording. Keep important original files and check the result before using it.

The maximum duration is 30 minutes per file, for both free and PRO use. Supported inputs also depend on the formats the device can decode. Processing speed depends on the device, file and available resources.
